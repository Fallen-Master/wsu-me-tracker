Why this says 38% and not 55%

The old version divided every credit you'd ever earned by 128. That's the wrong math. WSU only counts a credit once it fills a specific degree requirement.

This tracker now uses WSU's method: credits that fill a requirement ÷ 128. That's why it matches your transfer portal.
